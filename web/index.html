    What proven means

    Proven
    Both sides record the same key. The bridge deposit names the origin transaction; the NEAR receipts name the account, the token, the amount and the destination.
    Confirmed
    The bridge names the payout transaction, and we read it on its own chain paying your address: the bridge's report, checked against that chain. This is how payouts are checked on 33 chains, from Bitcoin and Solana to TON, XRP and Starknet.
    Matched
    No bridge record yet, so we search the destination's history (Ethereum, Optimism, Gnosis, Bitcoin, Solana and TRON) for the one transfer that fits the amount, less the bridge fee, and the time. PoA payouts come from a custodial wallet with no on-chain link back, so that is a match, not a proof.
    Recorded
    The Omni bridge's indexer has not named the payout yet, but the bridge's own contract on the destination chain records the transfer as paid. It sets that mark only in a payout that checked the bridge's signature and paid the signed recipient.
    Shielded
    A Zcash payout into a shielded pool: the transaction the bridge names is confirmed on Zcash and moved the withdrawal, less fees, into that pool.
    Unverified
    A hop we could not check: a chain we cannot read yet (Movement, Aleo, Quantus, Fogo), or a source that did not answer. We say why instead of guessing.
    Stuck
    Funds credited to the order account with no intent executed for over an hour. Most swaps settle in seconds.
    Refunded
    The deposited token left the account without a swap. Status APIs sometimes report these as successes.
