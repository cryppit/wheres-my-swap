Ask the bot before you send.

The same check, in a chat. Send the bot an address and it answers in plain words, then sends a picture of the answer you can forward to anyone.

  1. 1

    Open the bot

    Search for @WheresMySwap_Bot in Telegram, or use the button below, and press Start.

  2. 2

    Send /address and the address

    Add the chain you are sending on at the end, if you like:

    /address tex1s2rt77ggv6q989lr49rkgzmh5slsksa9khdgte zcash

    In a private chat with the bot you can also just paste the address, with no command.

  3. 3

    Read the answer

    Which chain it belongs to, whether it has a typo, and for Zcash whether a transparent-only or a shielded wallet can pay it, then the picture. If you named a chain and the address belongs to another, it says Stop.

Open @WheresMySwap_Bot ↗

Chain words it knows: zcash, bitcoin, ethereum (or base, arbitrum, optimism, polygon, bnb, hyperliquid), solana, tron, xrp, stellar, ton, dogecoin, near, litecoin, bch, dash, cardano, sui, aptos, starknet, fogo, aleo, movement, quantus. Short names work too, like btc, eth, sol or ltc. The bot reads only the address itself: nothing is looked up or kept.

Independent of NEAR Intents and the wallets that use it. Checks read public data in your browser and store nothing; Flight Recorder keeps only the watchlists you create.
