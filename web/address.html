Where’s My Swap Address check

Can your wallet send to this address?

Paste an address before you send. You’ll see which chain it belongs to, whether it has a typo, and in plain words what to do. For Zcash it also tells you whether a transparent-only wallet or a shielded wallet can pay it.

Checked in your browser. The addresses are never sent anywhere.

This checks an address’s form, offline. It can’t tell who controls an address or whether it’s the one you meant: compare it with the source.
